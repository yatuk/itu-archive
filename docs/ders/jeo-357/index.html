Ders içeriği

İngilizce adı: Experimental Rock Mechanics.

Principles of rock mechanics are introduced. Fundamental terms, concepts and definitions in rock engineering are explained. Physical and mechanical properties of rock materials are taught. Rock failure criteria are presented. Effects on the mechanical behavior of rock materials are introduced. Mechanical, hydraulic and coupled hydromechanical properties of discontinuities are introduced. Laboratory testing methods of rock materials & discontinuities are presented. Skills for designing experimental set-up, performing tests, and interpreting experimental data are given to students. Rock mechanics in situ tests are also introduced.

Öğrenme çıktıları

  • communicate with mining and geotechnical engineers through a common occupational language
  • have knowledge on fundamental terms, concepts and definitions used in rock engineering
  • classify materials forming the Earth’s crust in terms of geo-engineering
  • know general properties of rock masses as well as the distinction between rock material and rock mass terms
  • have knowledge on physical properties and stress – strain behaviors of rock materials
  • apprehend failure criteria in rock mechanics and factors affecting mechanical behavior of rocks
  • learn mechanical, hydraulic and coupled hydro-mechanical behaviors of discontinuities
  • know rock material & discontinuity testing methods commonly conducted in rock mechanics laboratories
  • have skill to design experimental set-up for the purpose of rock and discontinuity testing in laboratory
  • recognize in situ tests performed in rock masses
  • read and interpret data and plots obtained from both laboratory and in situ tests.
  • have experience for the preparation of laboratory reports as well as presentation of them
